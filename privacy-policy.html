Patron Discretion Standard

Privacy Policy & Patron Data Stewardship

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Ginger Meal Max, Manhattan Dining Salon

1. Introduction & Dining Discretion Mandate

At Ginger Meal Max, operating our fine dining salon and culinary hearth atelier at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, we hold guest confidentiality, custom dietary allergen dossiers, and patron privacy to the highest standards of luxury hospitality. We recognize that reserving tasting tables, booking private chef's counter seatings, or ordering curated ginger condiment sets involves sharing personal contact information and confidential billing records. This Privacy Policy outlines the exact protocols we employ to collect, process, safeguard, and retain your data across our digital portals and Manhattan establishment.

2. Categories of Information Collected

When you engage with our dining salon, we collect essential guest information including your full name, email address, telephone number, billing records, dietary allergy profiles, and wine pairing preferences recorded during private dinner seatings. Additionally, when you utilize our interactive course configurator or explore our culinary research journal, our web servers record technical telemetry such as IP addresses, browser types, device identifiers, and referral paths via Google Tag manager. These technical metrics enable our digital culinary engineers to optimize browsing performance and protect transaction security across international patrons.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Data Processing

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate artisan culinary hospitality operations, including managing table seating allocations, preparing customized allergen-free ginger menus, coordinating sommelier wine pairings, generating formal invoices, and delivering private dining confirmation updates. Furthermore, with your explicit consent, we may send private invitations to seasonal tasting menu unveilings and rare vintage cellar releases. We maintain an absolute policy never to sell, rent, monetize, or publicly distribute patron identities or dietary dossiers to any third-party marketing brokers.

4. Data Storage & Cryptographic Protection Standards

All digital reservation records, private tasting preferences, and billing information entrusted to Ginger Meal Max are stored on private cloud servers shielded by 256-bit AES encryption protocols and strict administrative access controls. Digital payment transactions are handled directly through certified, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant merchant processors, ensuring our salon never retains raw credit card numbers on local hardware. We perform continuous vulnerability assessments and cybersecurity audits to guarantee that your private information remains completely protected from unauthorized external access.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Access Controls

Regardless of your country of residence, Ginger Meal Max extends comprehensive data privacy rights aligned with global standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You retain the absolute legal right to inspect the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to profile records, restrict processing activities, or demand the permanent deletion of your reservation records from our systems. To exercise these statutory privacy rights, simply submit a formal request to our maître d' concierge desk at any time.

6. Data Retention Protocols & Archival Integrity

We retain client dining history and custom tasting notes for as long as your patron profile remains active, enabling seamless repeat visits, personalized menu customization, and preferred table allocations without requiring repetitive intake questions. If you choose to conclude your patronage or request data expungement, all electronic records and custom dietary preferences are securely purged and permanently deleted within thirty business days, subject only to mandatory statutory tax and accounting retention requirements mandated by United States commercial law.

7. Atelier Contact for Privacy Governance

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to discuss our client confidentiality practices with our leadership team, please contact our administrative desk. Written correspondence should be addressed directly to Ginger Meal Max, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted telephonically to our concierge staff by calling +1-888-777-5845 during standard business hours. Our compliance committee will examine your communication with professional care and respond within standard commercial timelines.
