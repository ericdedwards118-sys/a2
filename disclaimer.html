Dining & Culinary Transparency Disclaimer

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 • Ginger Meal Max, Manhattan Dining Salon

1. General Dining Notice & Information Scope

The culinary treatises, gingerol chemistry breakdowns, confit poaching formulas, and sommelier wine recommendations published across the Ginger Meal Max digital platforms and at our flagship salon located at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, are presented exclusively for general educational, historical, and fine dining appreciation. While our master chefs make every reasonable effort to ensure that all published recipes reflect verified culinary science, Ginger Meal Max notes that individual sensory perception involves subjective palate variables.

2. Natural Rhizome Characteristics & Pungency Variations

Natural ginger root is an agricultural harvest of the *Zingiber officinale* plant. As an authentic agricultural crop, raw ginger rhizomes may exhibit subtle variations in gingerol concentration, essential oleoresin depth, and natural fibrous density that distinguish real organic crops from synthetic chemical flavorings. These micro-characteristics represent authentic hallmarks of seasonal gastronomy and should not be construed as culinary imperfections.

3. Food Allergen Transparency & Cross-Contact Protocols

Our culinary kitchen prepares tasting menus featuring tree nuts, seafood, dairy, and fermented wild yeast cultures alongside fresh ginger rhizomes. While our kitchen team enforces rigorous allergen segregation and clean cooking surfaces, airborne microscopic cross-contact may occur during active dinner service. Patrons with severe anaphylactic sensitivities must inform our maître d' prior to arrival so that specialized preparation protocols can be enacted.

4. Spice Tolerance & Digestive Considerations

Our tasting courses showcase a dynamic range of ginger preparations, from mellow, sweet honey confits to fiery raw rhizome reductions and effervescent fermented ginger bugs. While ginger is celebrated for promoting gastrointestinal comfort, individuals with acute gastric sensitivities or specific medical dietary restrictions are advised to consume rich spicy courses in moderation according to personal tolerance.

5. Scientific References & Culinary Citations

Our editorial journal frequently cites gastronomy institutes, university botanical laboratories, and historical food archives to provide cultural and cellular context for our patrons. These scholarly citations are curated solely to enhance your culinary appreciation and environmental understanding. Ginger Meal Max exercises no administrative authority, editorial control, or technical oversight over third-party gastronomy institutes, and we assume no legal liability for external research publications.

6. Limitation of Salon Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable statutory law, Ginger Meal Max, its chefs, sommeliers, and affiliates shall not be held liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from undisclosed patron food allergies, third-party carrier delays for packaged condiments, or dietary intolerances. Our total aggregate liability concerning any verified commercial dispute or dining claim shall be strictly limited to the verified purchase price paid by the guest for the specific dining service.

7. Atelier Contact for Dining Inquiries

Should you require formal clarification regarding this disclaimer or wish to submit an official inquiry regarding our organic ginger certificates of origin, allergen protocols, or private chef's table bookings, please reach out to our concierge desk. Written correspondence should be addressed directly to Ginger Meal Max, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or submitted telephonically by calling +1-888-777-5845 during standard salon hours.
